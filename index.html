1. Overview

Sparkle ("the app") is operated by Foresightbytes ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Sparkle collects, why, and how it's handled. It applies to anyone who creates a Sparkle account.

2. Sparkle is a research pilot

Sparkle is currently running a short research study on whether creative activities — dance, poetry, and writing — help make stress and mood easier to manage. When you sign up you're asked to agree to take part before any study data is collected.

Sparkle is a self-assessment and creative wellness tool, not a diagnostic or treatment service. If you have concerns about your wellbeing at any time, please contact a qualified professional. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for free, confidential crisis support, 24 hours a day.

3. Information we collect

Everything below is tied to your account and only collected once you're signed in.

Account information

  • Email address and password — used to create and secure your account (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in plain text).
  • Full name — collected at signup.

Optional profile details

  • Profile photo, phone number, and date of birth — all optional, added only if you fill them in on the Edit Profile screen.

Wellbeing survey responses

  • Answers to standard, validated screening questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7) plus one study-specific question about confidence starting a creative activity, completed once at the start (baseline) and again after two weeks (follow-up), along with a few follow-up-only questions about your experience using the app.
  • Quick 1–5 mood check-ins you complete right after a dance, poetry, or writing session.

Content you create

  • Dance videos, and poetry or writing pieces you create or save inside the app.

App usage

  • Session counts, time spent viewing versus creating, and how many pieces you start or finish — used to understand engagement with the app during the pilot. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Device permissions

4. How we use it

  • To run your account and let you sign in securely.
  • To analyze whether creative activities affect self-reported stress, anxiety, and mood over the course of the pilot.
  • To show you your own progress over time on your Profile screen.
  • To generate AI-assisted writing/poetry feedback within a session (see below).
  • To improve the app based on how it's actually used.

6. Retention & withdrawal

You can withdraw from the study at any time from your Profile screen. Withdrawing stops any further participation immediately; data already collected up to that point is retained but excluded from analysis — standard practice for research integrity, since removing it after the fact could bias the study's results.

7. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted by server-side security rules to your own account and to study administrators who need it to run the pilot.

8. Children's privacy

Sparkle is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
